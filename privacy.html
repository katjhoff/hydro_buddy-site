SipWorld

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8, October 2026

Pocket Engine Studio, LLC ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, use, and protect your information within the SipWorld application. SipWorld is operated from the United States and data is processed in the United States. SipWorld is not offered in, and is not intended for use in, the European Union (EU), United Kingdom (UK), Switzerland, Brazil, or Thailand.

1. Information We Collect & How We Use It
  • Account & Identity: We silently create an anonymous account (Firebase UID) on launch. If you choose to link Google or Apple Sign-In, we collect the associated email address and name to secure your account.
  • App Hydration Data: Your hydration history logged directly in SipWorld (daily goal, ml logged, volume/sip streaks, XP, selected world, and reminder times) is saved to our secure cloud database (Firestore) so your streaks and Squad progress function correctly.
  • Location Data: With your consent, we collect approximate and precise location coordinates. These are processed ephemerally on our servers and sent to OpenWeatherMap to calculate your weather-adjusted hydration goal. We do not store your location history.
  • Weight & Activity Level: If entered during onboarding to calculate your goal, this data is stored strictly on your device and is never uploaded to our servers.
  • Camera & Photos ("Today's Snap"): The camera is only used when you explicitly tap it. Photos taken for "Today's Snap" are stored locally on your device, are never uploaded to our servers, and are deleted if you uninstall the app.
  • Device & Analytics Data: We collect push notification tokens (FCM), IP addresses, crash logs, and diagnostic data via Firebase Crashlytics to monitor app health and deliver features (like Squad Nudges).
  • Advertising IDs: AdMob collects standard advertising identifiers (IDFA/AAID) to serve rewarded ads to free users.
  • Purchase Data: We collect your purchase history and subscription status to unlock Pro features.
2. Apple HealthKit & Android Health Connect

Limited Use Disclosure: Information received from Apple HealthKit and Android Health Connect is used strictly to provide the app's core hydration tracking features. It is read and written on-device only.

  • Raw data pulled from Health platforms is never transmitted to our servers, never sold, and never used for advertising.
  • It is not transferred to third parties except as strictly needed to provide the feature.
  • You can revoke access at any time in your device's Health settings.
3. Squad Data, Nudges & Public Visibility

If you join a Squad, specific data points are shared to facilitate group motivation.

  • What Squad members see: Your auto-generated explorer display name, world theme, sip streak, daily progress ring, your daily goal, and the total ml logged today.
  • What they NEVER see: Your XP, volume streak, photos, or raw Health app data.
  • Nudges: When sending a nudge, our server briefly processes sender/recipient IDs to deliver the push notification, retains a daily count to prevent spam, and then deletes the ephemeral nudge record.
4. Third-Party Processors & Wearables

We utilize industry-standard services to operate the App, all of which use encryption in transit:

  • Google (Firebase/Firestore/Crashlytics/FCM): For database, auth, crash reporting, and push notifications.
  • Apple & Google (Sign-in & Payments): For optional OAuth sign-in and secure payment processing.
  • RevenueCat: Manages subscription entitlements (receives Firebase UID, purchase history, and IP address).
  • OpenWeatherMap: Ephemerally processes location coordinates to return weather data.
  • AdMob (Google): Serves ads and provides consent management (UMP).
  • Wearables & Widgets: Apple Watch, Wear OS, and home-screen widgets communicate with SipWorld via Apple and Google's secure on-device channels.
5. Data Retention & How to Delete Your Account

Anonymous accounts persist until deleted. You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.

  • In-App Deletion: Navigate to Settings > Delete Account within the SipWorld app.
  • Web/Email Deletion: If you have uninstalled the app, email develop@pocketenginestudio.com with your User ID to request manual deletion within 30 days.

Exceptions: Upon deletion, your cloud data is wiped. However, a minimal "tombstone" record (UID and timestamp) is retained to prevent purchase fraud. RevenueCat purchase records, Google/Apple transaction receipts, and anonymized Crashlytics logs are retained per the respective processors' retention policies. Water entries previously written to Apple Health/Health Connect remain in your device's health ecosystem.

6. US State Privacy Rights (CCPA & Others)

For residents of California and other applicable US states: We collect identifiers, precise geolocation (sensitive personal info), health-adjacent hydration data (sensitive personal info), and commercial purchase information to provide our service. We do not use sensitive information beyond providing the core service. AdMob may share identifiers to serve personalized ads. You may opt out of personalized tracking via your device settings (ATT on iOS or Ad Personalization on Android) or the in-app "Your Privacy Choices" menu. You have the right to know, correct, and delete your data, and we will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

7. Children's Privacy

SipWorld is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If we become aware we have collected such data, we will delete it.

8. Changes to this Policy & Contact Us

We may update this policy periodically. We will notify you of significant changes via in-app notices or push notifications.
